(published-table readers), `code/models.py` (RF model IO + pooling).

## Reconstructed here

Two steps had no surviving script; both were rebuilt and then verified against the
outputs they were supposed to have produced:

* `make_rankscore.py` — matches all 56 shipped rankscore files to **1.1e-16**.
* `literature_validation.py` — reproduces all 26 marker rows exactly (percentile
  delta 0.00, training-isolate counts identical, calls identical).
